## 1. Amendment Notice

This document is a proposed amendment package submitted under the Amendment Process of the Mirleen Constitution. It does not amend, replace, reinterpret, or create an exception to the Constitution by its existence, publication, review, or informal acceptance.

The Constitution shall remain unchanged unless the Project Owner explicitly ratifies this amendment. Silence, discussion, repository presence, implementation work, or merger of this proposal shall not constitute ratification.

If ratified, the exact changes specified in this document shall be incorporated into `governance/MIRLEEN_CONSTITUTION.md` through a distinct, traceable constitutional commit. Any modification to the proposed text before ratification shall be reflected in a revised version of this proposal and reviewed as part of the amendment decision.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of MCA-001 is to strengthen the Constitution so that it can govern the Mirleen Engineering System as a durable engineering operating system for human engineers and AI-assisted engineering work.

The amendment expressly establishes:

1. the Mirleen Engineering System as the organizational system within which governance, philosophy, policies, standards, procedures, knowledge, and evidence operate;
2. Engineering Philosophy as a non-normative interpretive layer immediately below the Constitution;
3. Engineering Humility as a binding constitutional engineering principle;
4. Single Source of Truth as a binding constitutional requirement;
5. end-to-end governance and engineering traceability;
6. validated recovery capability as distinct from an untested recovery plan;
7. a precise separation between principles, policies, standards, procedures, templates, registers, decision records, repository documentation, and daily instructions; and
8. the required closing identity of every official Mirleen governance document.

## 3. Problem and Opportunity

The current Constitution already establishes strong foundations for supremacy, documentation, reversibility, recovery, human accountability, evidence, traceability, proportional governance, and continuous learning. It does not yet expressly define the Mirleen Engineering System, recognize a non-normative Engineering Philosophy layer, establish Single Source of Truth as an independent constitutional principle, or define full traceability from constitutional authority through implementation evidence and repository truth.

The Constitution also requires honest treatment of uncertainty but does not yet state the expected conduct of AI engineering assistants with sufficient specificity. Because Mirleen intends its engineering system to remain trustworthy across changing teams, technologies, and AI systems, this expectation should be constitutionally explicit.

This amendment provides an opportunity to establish these concepts before dependent policies, standards, procedures, templates, registers, decision systems, repository baselines, and knowledge structures are created. Early clarification reduces future ambiguity, duplication, migration cost, and governance drift.

## 4. Design Constraints

This amendment is governed by the following constraints:

1. The Constitution remains the supreme governing authority.
2. Engineering Philosophy explains constitutional intent but creates no mandatory requirements.
3. Normative requirements remain owned by exactly one authoritative source.
4. The Recovery Doctrine may explain why recovery matters but shall not duplicate the binding recovery requirements owned by the Constitution, policies, and standards.
5. Human accountability shall not be transferred to an AI system.
6. Traceability shall improve verification and accountability without creating duplicate authority.
7. Continuous improvement shall not weaken constitutional stability or bypass the Amendment Process.
8. Existing constitutional protections shall be preserved unless this amendment expressly strengthens or clarifies them.

## 5. Exact Constitutional Text Proposed

### 5.1 Add a Mirleen Engineering System section

Insert the following section immediately after `## Mission` and its existing content and before `## Core Values`:

> ## The Mirleen Engineering System
>
> The Mirleen Engineering System, known as MES, is the enduring engineering operating system through which Mirleen translates constitutional purpose into trustworthy engineering practice, preserves knowledge, and maintains accountable, recoverable, and continuously improving systems.
>
> MES comprises the Constitution, Engineering Philosophy, Governance Framework, Engineering Policies, Engineering Standards, Operating Procedures, Templates, Registers, Decision Records, Repository Baselines, and Knowledge Base, together with the traceable relationships among them.
>
> Governance is one subsystem of MES and shall not be mistaken for the whole system. MES shall preserve the distinction between authority, interpretation, obligation, control, execution, evidence, rationale, repository-specific truth, and non-normative knowledge.
>
> MES shall remain understandable, maintainable, recoverable, and trustworthy across changes in personnel, repositories, technologies, programming languages, frameworks, vendors, and artificial intelligence systems. Engineering knowledge shall be preserved so that it can outlive any individual implementation without becoming detached from its governing authority or evidentiary basis.

### 5.2 Extend Article III — Human responsibility

Append the following clauses to `### Article III — Human responsibility`:

> 5. AI engineering assistants shall distinguish verified facts from assumptions, conclusions from recommendations, and evidence from inference.
> 6. AI engineering assistants shall communicate material uncertainty honestly, shall not fabricate certainty, and shall not overstate confidence.
> 7. Apparent confidence, speed, fluency, or convenience shall not take precedence over engineering integrity, truthful representation, appropriate verification, or human accountability.
> 8. A human decision-maker remains responsible for evaluating AI-assisted output to a degree proportionate to the impact, uncertainty, security exposure, and reversibility of the affected work.

### 5.3 Extend Article IX — Traceability and transparency

Append the following clauses to `### Article IX — Traceability and transparency`:

> 5. Significant engineering requirements and decisions shall be traceable, as applicable, from their constitutional basis through governing policies, standards, procedures, templates, implementation evidence, decision records, and repository documentation.
> 6. Every normative governance document shall identify its governing authority, governing references, direct dependencies, and known dependent document classes.
> 7. Traceability shall distinguish proposed, approved, implemented, verified, superseded, withdrawn, and rejected states wherever those distinctions are material.
> 8. Traceability shall support accountability, verification, recovery, impact analysis, and maintainability without creating duplicate sources of authority.
> 9. A traceability link communicates relationship and provenance; it does not transfer, duplicate, or redefine the authority of the referenced source.

### 5.4 Add a constitutional article for Single Source of Truth

Insert the following new article immediately after `### Article IX — Traceability and transparency` and before the current `### Article X — Sustainable delivery`:

> ### Article X — Single Source of Truth and normative integrity
>
> 1. Every normative requirement shall have exactly one authoritative source and an identifiable owner.
> 2. A document that depends upon a normative requirement shall reference its authoritative source and shall not duplicate, redefine, weaken, or independently reinterpret that requirement.
> 3. Policies, standards, procedures, templates, registers, decision records, repository documentation, guidance, examples, and daily instructions shall preserve their distinct responsibilities and shall not assume authority assigned to another document class.
> 4. Summaries, examples, templates, guidance, and knowledge materials shall be clearly identified as non-authoritative wherever they could reasonably be mistaken for the source of a requirement.
> 5. When an authoritative requirement changes, dependent documents shall be reviewed for compatibility. They shall be changed only where their own references, controls, execution guidance, structure, or implementation-specific content is affected.
> 6. No document may assign itself authority belonging to another document, redefine the authority of a higher-level document, or create an alternative source for an existing normative requirement.
> 7. Conflicting representations of the same requirement constitute a governance defect. The higher-authority or expressly designated authoritative source shall govern until the conflict is corrected.
> 8. Mirleen systems and repositories shall identify the authoritative location of material requirements, decisions, architecture, operational knowledge, recovery information, and repository-specific truth.

If this article is ratified:

1. the current Article X shall be renumbered Article XI;
2. the current Article XI shall be renumbered Article XII;
3. the current Article XII shall be renumbered Article XIII; and
4. all affected internal references shall be updated, including Article I clause 4, which shall refer to Article XIII.

No other article shall be renumbered or altered by implication.

### 5.5 Add Engineering Humility to Engineering Principles

Append the following item to `## Engineering Principles`:

> 11. **Practice engineering humility.** Engineers and AI-assisted engineering systems shall communicate uncertainty honestly; distinguish verified facts from assumptions, conclusions from recommendations, and evidence from inference; and shall never fabricate certainty. Apparent confidence shall not take precedence over engineering integrity, truthful representation, or appropriate verification.

### 5.6 Strengthen Recovery & Rollback Principles

Append the following item to `## Recovery & Rollback Principles`:

> 9. A recovery plan that has not been tested or otherwise validated to a degree proportionate to its risk shall not be represented or relied upon as a verified recovery capability. Where pre-implementation testing is not reasonably possible, that limitation, the evidence available, the residual risk, and the compensating safeguards shall be documented and approved by the authority appropriate to the risk.

This clause does not replace Article V or the existing Recovery & Rollback Principles. It clarifies the evidentiary threshold for claiming that a recovery capability is verified.

### 5.7 Replace the Authority Levels section

Replace the complete existing `## Authority Levels` section, from its heading through the paragraph immediately preceding `## Amendment Process`, with the following text:

> ## Authority and System Layers
>
> Mirleen distinguishes binding authority, non-normative interpretation, implementation guidance, and historical evidence. A document’s position in MES shall not be used to claim authority that its document class does not possess.
>
> ### Level 1 — Constitutional Authority
>
> This Constitution is supreme and binding across Mirleen engineering. Only an explicitly ratified constitutional amendment or an explicit, documented Project Owner exception may alter its application.
>
> ### Interpretive Layer — Engineering Philosophy
>
> Engineering Philosophy is positioned immediately below the Constitution conceptually, but it is not an authority level and is not a source of mandatory requirements. It explains constitutional purpose, culture, doctrines, engineering mindset, and long-term intent. It shall remain consistent with the Constitution and shall refer to authoritative requirements without duplicating or redefining them.
>
> ### Level 2 — Policy Authority
>
> Organization-wide policies and formally approved governance directives define binding obligations and required organizational outcomes. They derive from the Constitution and shall remain consistent with it.
>
> ### Level 3 — Engineering Standards Authority
>
> Engineering standards define mandatory, measurable, or objectively verifiable controls that satisfy policies. They shall identify their governing policies and constitutional basis and shall not independently redefine policy outcomes.
>
> ### Level 4 — Operational and Repository Authority
>
> Procedures define ordered execution. Repository policies, architectural decisions, runbooks, controls, and operating agreements govern a defined project, system, or domain. They shall conform to all higher authorities and shall preserve repository-specific truth without duplicating organization-wide requirements.
>
> ### Level 5 — Implementation Guidance and Structure
>
> Templates define evidence structure. Guides, conventions, examples, recommendations, and daily instructions support consistent execution. They do not create authority and cannot override binding requirements.
>
> ### Evidentiary Layer — Registers and Records
>
> Registers preserve governance state. Decision Records preserve rationale. Change, recovery, incident, review, and other records preserve historical evidence. Records and registers do not create normative requirements merely because they have been approved, accepted, or retained.
>
> ### Knowledge Layer — Preserved Engineering Knowledge
>
> The Knowledge Base preserves non-normative concepts, explanations, lessons, and guidance independently of any single repository, technology, framework, or AI system. It shall identify authoritative sources and shall not become an alternative source of normative requirements.
>
> Principles explain values and intent. Policies define organizational obligations. Standards define measurable engineering controls. Procedures define ordered execution. Templates define evidence structure. Registers preserve state. Decision Records preserve rationale. Repository Documentation preserves repository-specific truth. Daily Engineering Instructions guide immediate work within all higher authorities.
>
> A lower-authority document may impose more specific or stronger controls within its legitimate scope but may not weaken a higher-level obligation. When authorities at the same level conflict, the more specific provision governs within its legitimate scope only if it does not weaken another binding obligation and the designated authority has not resolved otherwise. When authorities at different levels conflict, the higher authority governs. Ambiguity shall be escalated rather than resolved through silent noncompliance.

## 6. Reason for the Amendment

Mirleen is establishing an engineering system intended to endure beyond individual repositories, technologies, teams, and AI models. The Constitution must therefore distinguish the complete MES from its governance subsystem and protect the relationships among authority, philosophy, obligations, controls, execution, evidence, rationale, repository truth, and preserved knowledge.

Explicit constitutional treatment is necessary because Single Source of Truth, traceability, Engineering Humility, and document-class boundaries affect every future MES subsystem. Leaving them implicit would permit inconsistent interpretations and increase the possibility that later documents duplicate requirements, confuse guidance with authority, treat unverified recovery plans as established capabilities, or represent AI-generated confidence as evidence.

The amendment strengthens the Constitution while preserving its original vision, mission, values, and safeguards.

## 7. Expected Organizational and Technical Effects

If ratified, this amendment will:

1. establish MES as the organizing model for Mirleen engineering;
2. authorize creation of a non-normative Engineering Philosophy layer without allowing that layer to create requirements;
3. require governance documents to declare relationships needed for traceability;
4. require normative requirements to have a single authoritative source and owner;
5. cause policies, standards, procedures, templates, registers, decision records, repository documentation, and knowledge materials to maintain strict responsibility boundaries;
6. require truthful uncertainty communication in human and AI-assisted engineering;
7. prevent untested or insufficiently validated recovery plans from being represented as verified recovery capabilities;
8. require official governance documents to preserve both the opening and closing Mirleen documentation identity;
9. support compatibility and impact review when authoritative sources change; and
10. reduce duplication, governance drift, false confidence, and loss of institutional knowledge.

- Engineering Humility strengthens Core Values 1 and 3 and Articles III and VII.

No constitutional exception is requested or created by this amendment.

## 10. Risks and Mitigations

### Risk 1 — Philosophy may be mistaken for policy

**Mitigation:** The proposed authority model explicitly declares Engineering Philosophy non-normative and prohibits it from creating mandatory requirements.

### Risk 2 — Traceability may become excessive ceremony

**Mitigation:** Article XI on governance proportionality, if renumbered from the current Article X, and the existing Governance Principles continue to require proportionality. Traceability depth shall be proportional to significance and risk.

### Risk 3 — Single Source of Truth may be confused with a single physical repository

**Mitigation:** The proposed article governs authoritative ownership, not physical centralization. Repository-specific truth may remain in its owning repository when its authoritative location is identifiable.

### Risk 4 — References may become stale

**Mitigation:** The planned governance lifecycle and traceability controls shall require compatibility review when authoritative sources change.

### Risk 5 — Recovery validation language may be impracticable for novel systems

**Mitigation:** The proposed clause permits proportionate alternative validation when pre-implementation testing is not reasonably possible, provided limitations, residual risk, safeguards, and approval are documented.

### Risk 6 — Renumbering may break internal references

**Mitigation:** Ratification implementation shall include a complete internal-reference check. Article I clause 4 and any other affected references shall be updated in the same constitutional commit.

### Risk 7 — The MES definition may encourage uncontrolled document growth

**Mitigation:** Single Source of Truth, strict document-class responsibilities, proportional governance, and modular ownership limit duplication and unnecessary artifacts.
