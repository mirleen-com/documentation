# Mirleen Constitution
# دستور ميرلين

> **The foundational constitution that governs every platform, every project, every document, every decision, and every line of code developed within Mirleen.**
>
> **الدستور التأسيسي الذي يحكم كل منصة، وكل مشروع، وكل وثيقة، وكل قرار، وكل سطر برمجي يُطوَّر داخل ميرلين.**

---

# Introduction | المقدمة

Mirleen was not established as a business seeking products.

It emerged naturally from a real engineering need.

While building the **Super Console Platform**, we realized that sustainable software requires more than code. It requires documentation, architecture, engineering standards, automation, and disciplined decision-making.

This realization led to the creation of the **AI Engineering Platform**.

As the platform matured, it became clear that it represented the engineering foundation for every future system.

From this foundation, Mirleen was established.

Our mission is to reduce the distance between ideas and implementation, automate engineering wherever possible, and make technology a natural extension of human creativity and capability.

Every step in this journey has been by the grace of Allah.

---

لم تُنشأ ميرلين كشركة تبحث عن منتجات.

بل نشأت بصورة طبيعية من حاجة هندسية حقيقية.

أثناء تطوير **Super Console Platform** أدركنا أن البرمجيات المستدامة تحتاج إلى أكثر من كتابة الشيفرة، بل تحتاج إلى توثيق، ومعمارية، ومعايير هندسية، وأتمتة، ومنهجية واضحة لاتخاذ القرار.

ومن هذه الحاجة نشأت **AI Engineering Platform**.

ومع نضوجها أصبحت الأساس الهندسي الذي ستُبنى عليه جميع الأنظمة المستقبلية.

ومن هذا الأساس وُلدت **Mirleen**.

غايتنا هي تقليل المسافة بين الفكرة والتنفيذ، وأتمتة العمل الهندسي حيثما أمكن، وجعل التقنية امتدادًا طبيعيًا لقدرات الإنسان وإبداعه.

وما كان ذلك إلا بفضل الله.

# Article I — Our Principles
# المادة الأولى — مبادئنا

1. We never sacrifice quality for speed.
2. We never hide mistakes; we document them and learn from them.
3. We never write what we do not understand.
4. Every important decision must be explainable.
5. Honesty, trust, and excellence guide everything we build.
6. Humans are responsible for vision, understanding, and decisions. Systems are responsible for execution, verification, and repetition within clearly defined boundaries.
7. Official project documentation is the source of truth—not conversations or memory.
8. Every important decision must be documented, and every significant change must remain traceable.
9. No AI Agent operates without a defined objective, permissions, boundaries, and stopping conditions.
10. Safety, understanding, and quality always take precedence over speed.

# المادة الرابعة — المصدر الرسمي

Official Mirleen documentation is the only authoritative source for knowledge, architecture, standards, and engineering decisions.
